Biography

I am an MRes student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. I am also an Agent Application R&D Intern at LuckyShort, where I work on video-algorithm applications and agent workflows for AI-native content production.

I work on trustworthy artificial intelligence. My research interests broadly lie in multimodal large language models, numerical and time-series reasoning, reliable forecasting, and generative media systems. I am particularly interested in models that can recognize uncertainty, explain their reasoning, and remain dependable when real-world data is noisy or unfamiliar.

Previously, I was an Algorithm R&D Intern at the International Digital Economy Academy (IDEA) and a Research Assistant at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). My work has appeared at venues including ICML, ACM Multimedia, ICRA, ICASSP, IEEE Sensors Journal, and Pattern Recognition.

I am open to research collaborations and engineering discussions on trustworthy AI, multimodal reasoning, time-series intelligence, and agentic content systems.
