About

Research rigor, built for the real world.

My research asks a practical question: how can AI systems remain useful when the data is noisy, incomplete, unfamiliar, or constantly changing?

I began in machine olfaction and graph learning, moved into reliable time-series forecasting and multimodal language-model reasoning, and now also build agentic video-production systems. This path gives me a broad view of the full stack—from research ideas and controlled experiments to production reliability and user feedback.

Research focus

Three connected directions.

I focus on methods that make intelligent systems more dependable, interpretable, and useful outside ideal laboratory conditions.

01

Trustworthy AI

Forecasting models that recognize ambiguity, reject unfamiliar futures, and remain stable under perturbations instead of producing confident but unreliable predictions.

  • Uncertainty
  • Robustness
  • Faithfulness
02

Multimodal Reasoning

Models that connect numerical signals with language, infer structured events, and provide intermediate reasoning that people can inspect.

  • LLM post-training
  • GRPO
  • Number-to-event
03

AI Content Systems

Reliable agent workflows for script understanding, character assets, video generation, music, quality control, and high-volume creative delivery.

  • Agents
  • Video generation
  • Production tooling
